SeeRead is a free, open-source teleprompter for your desktop. It floats beside your camera and follows your voice, entirely on your own computer: no account, no cloud.

Download SeeRead Source on GitHub

Windows, macOS and Linux · Apache-2.0 · Early release

It follows your voice.
It never guesses ahead.

In Voice mode, SeeRead listens with speech recognition that runs on your computer. Every phrase you say is matched against the script near your place, so the text moves with you and nowhere else. Small steps forward need little evidence; bigger moves need several matching words.

  1. [silence]

    You stop to think. The text waits. Silence never moves it.

  2. “the same phrase twice”

    A line comes up again later in the script. SeeRead stays with the nearest match instead of leaping to the other one.

  3. “…and that's why we…”

    You skip a passage. After a few words that match further on, it catches up with you.

  4. “…as I said, our results…”

    You re-read a sentence. It goes back only when you clearly do.

  5. “keep your eyes on the lands”

    A word is misheard. Close matches still count, and one stray word never yanks the text.

  6. [click]

    You lose your place anyway. Click the word you're on, or press Left arrow / Right arrow to nudge.

Recognition uses the open-source sherpa-onnx engine with a bundled English model. Audio is processed in memory and thrown away. For other languages, add any sherpa-onnx streaming model folder.

No microphone?
It scrolls for you.

Auto-scroll moves the script at a steady pace you set, and the moment you scroll with the mouse wheel or the keyboard, you are in charge. Switching between Auto-scroll and Voice keeps your place.

  • Adjustable speed and acceleration, live with Up arrow / Down arrow
  • A countdown before it starts, and optional pauses between paragraphs
  • Loop back to the top or stop at the end

[Pause. Smile.]
Seen, never spoken.

Anything in square brackets is a stage direction. It stays on screen in its own colour, voice tracking skips right over it, and it never moves your place. 

Welcome, everyone. [Wait for the room to settle.]
Today I'll walk you through our results.
>> Slide 2
The short version: it worked.

Prefer other markers? Add ( ), { }, < > or any pair of your own, mark whole lines with a prefix such as >>, hide directions while you read, and use Export clean copy to save a version without them.

The script editor around it has what a script needs: open and save plain .txt and .md files, find and replace, undo, word count, a reading-time estimate, autosave and crash recovery.

It floats beside
your camera.

The teleprompter is its own see-through window that stays on top of your recorder, call or slides. Put it right under the webcam, drag it by its handle and resize it from the corner.

Mic on The microphone opens only while Voice mode or a microphone test is running, and this red indicator shows the whole time. Pausing keeps it open so you can resume instantly; Stop releases it.

Scripts are plain files you choose, settings stay in SeeRead's own folder, and the clipboard is touched only when you copy, cut or paste. Read the privacy statement.

Hiding the teleprompter from screen capture

SeeRead can ask your operating system to leave the teleprompter out of screenshots, recordings and screen sharing, then shows you what the system reports back.

System What to expect
Windows 10 (2004 or later) and 11 Hidden from most capture and sharing tools.
Older Windows 10 Shows up as a black rectangle instead.
macOS Best effort. Tools built on ScreenCaptureKit (macOS 15+) may still capture it.
Linux (X11 and Wayland) Not supported; the teleprompter appears in captures. On Wayland, share a single window instead of the whole screen.

This is never a guarantee. A camera filming the screen, remote-desktop sessions, driver-level capture, browser-based screen sharing and system updates can all still capture the window. Always check a test recording.

Questions, answered.

Is SeeRead free?

Yes. SeeRead is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0, and it needs no account.

Does SeeRead need an internet connection?

No. After you download it, SeeRead makes no network connections while it runs: no update checks, no analytics, no telemetry and no remote fonts.

Is my voice recorded or sent anywhere?

No. Audio is processed in memory by the speech engine on your computer and discarded immediately. It is never written to disk, logged or transmitted.

Which languages does voice tracking support?

An English model is bundled. You can add any sherpa-onnx streaming transducer model for other languages. Auto-scroll works with a script in any language.

Will the teleprompter show up in my recording or video call?

SeeRead can ask the operating system to leave the teleprompter out of captures. On Windows 10 version 2004 and later it is hidden from most capture and sharing tools; on macOS it is best effort; on Linux it is not supported. This is never a guarantee, so always check a test recording. Details by system.

Can I use SeeRead with a glass teleprompter rig?

Yes. The teleprompter can be mirrored horizontally, vertically or both.

Why does Windows or macOS warn me when I open the installer?

The installers are not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper warn on first launch. Every release lists SHA-256 checksums and carries build-provenance attestations so you can verify your download.

Can I control the teleprompter while another app has focus?

Yes. Global shortcuts start, pause, restart, stop and change speed while your recorder or video call has focus. On Linux they need an X11 or XWayland session; native Wayland does not allow them.

Ready when you are.

  • Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit · .exe or .msi
  • macOS 11 or later, Apple silicon and Intel · .dmg
  • Linux x86-64 · .AppImage or .deb

Installers are unsigned for now, so your system will warn on first launch. Check a download against the release's SHA256SUMS, or verify its provenance:
